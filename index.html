The supervision of a glass-tempering line (EN 12150-1): a C11 PLC drives a four-zone furnace, a gateway reads its measurements read-only, a data diode sends them one way to an API, then to a web console that shows each role what it is allowed to see.

Frames are signed (Ed25519, SHA-256) and numbered against replay.
